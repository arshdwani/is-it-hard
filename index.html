How this works

Every number is exact binomial maths, computed in your browser. There's no sampling and no real benchmark data. It assumes attempts are independent and the solve rate stays fixed, which real agents only roughly do.

"Hard" and "easy" mean 10% and 50% true solve rates here. The hidden-rate figure is a one-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson upper bound.
